## Taste DNA

### 1. Let one photograph carry the emotion
- **Trigger:** An investment fund page must feel safe, expensive and established before it says anything.
- **Decision:** One full-bleed photograph with a bottom gradient, no cards or icons above the fold.
- **Reason:** A real street at dusk signals "real assets" faster than a feature grid, and the gradient keeps the white type readable without a box behind it.
- **Evidence:** The hero frame is 100% photo plus three text elements; headline, tagline and button all sit in the lower 25%.

### 2. Serif for the promise, tiny capitals for everything else (Restraint)
- **Trigger:** A heading and its supporting copy compete for the same attention.
- **Decision:** Only the headline is large, and it is a serif; every other string is ~10px tracked capitals.
- **Reason:** A 6:1 size ratio leaves no doubt what to read first, and capitals at that size read as labels instead of body text. The cost: nothing longer than a short phrase can be set this way.
- **Evidence:** "UNLOCK THE TRUE POTENTIAL OF YOUR MULTI-FAMILY REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT" is two ~10px lines beside a ~70px two-line headline.

### 3. Hairline, not filled, for the call to action
- **Trigger:** A heavy filled button would pull attention off the photograph.
- **Decision:** A 1px outlined rectangle with a 2px radius.
- **Reason:** It reads as an invitation, which suits a high-value inquiry, and the square shape matches the architecture in the photo. The cost: low affordance for first-time visitors, offset by placing it at the end of the reading line.
- **Evidence:** "INVESTMENT INQUIRY" sits at the right end of the same baseline as the headline's last line.

### 4. Dark green, not black
- **Trigger:** Pure black next to a warm photo looks harsh.
- **Decision:** The mask panels and gradient end in a green-black (~#04120d) rather than #000.
- **Reason:** It picks up the greens of the trees in the photo, so the frame edge disappears into the image.
- **Evidence:** The intro frame's left and right panels are visibly green-tinted, fading to near black at the bottom.
